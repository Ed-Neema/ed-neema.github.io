About

Most AI safety work asks whether a model will refuse a harmful request. I am interested in a quieter failure: an agent given a harmless task that deletes a database, wipes a drive, or rewrites files nobody asked it to touch. I want agents whose actions stay proportionate to what was asked, and ways to measure when they do not.

I also work on healthcare AI for African languages. When a speech model mishears a drug name or a dose, the overall error rate barely moves, but the clinical meaning changes. I build datasets and evaluations that catch those errors.

Before research I worked as a full stack engineer, and I still like building the systems I study.

Research

2025 to now

Proportional action in tool using agents

How agents behave under ambiguity, risk and uncertainty, and inference time controls that keep their actions scoped to the task. I am building a benchmark that measures the changes an agent makes that the task did not require, scored by how reversible and how disproportionate they are.

2026 to now

BantuMed

A medical speech dataset and benchmark in Kinyarwanda, Swahili, Kirundi and Luganda, built from simulated clinician and patient consultations. It separates overall accuracy from errors that change clinical meaning, such as medication names, doses, symptoms and negation.

2025 to 2026

Surveillance for medical harms from LLM advice

A proposed reporting system for medical harms linked to advice from general purpose LLMs, combining a harm taxonomy, privacy aware data architecture and legal analysis across EU and US frameworks.

2026

Does retrieval help LLMs diagnose?

A controlled comparison of five retrieval setups on 60 MedQA cardiology questions. Hybrid retrieval gave the largest gain, single methods often hurt, and the reranker mainly guarded against bad retrievals. The differences were not yet significant at this sample size.

2025

A data lake for a digital agriculture platform

A change data capture pipeline streaming updates from microservice databases through Kafka into S3 with Delta Lake, for real time analytics on pest diagnosis and soil lab services.
